Install this as a Web App on your iPhone

The following instructions are for iPhone and Safari. The process is similar for Android and/or Chrome.

  • Make sure NOT to use an anonymous/private window when you browse to https://kx1t.com/commuterrail because those will prevent the system from remembering your last choice of transit line and stations!
  • Select the Transit authority, line, and stations you are interested in (see above)
  • Click the "Share" button. In Safari, it's the square with arrow button on the bottom of the screen. In Chrome, this button is in the address bar.
  • Select "Add to Home Screen". You may have to scroll down to see this
  • Change the name to something appropriate. Make sure that "Open as Web Page" is active. Then press "Add"

Done!

This application is an independent digital tool designed to help commuters track public transit schedules, route definitions, and real-time feeds across multiple municipal networks.

Privacy & Infrastructure Security

We believe in complete privacy. This platform is built from the ground up to protect your data with zero tracking.

  • Zero Personal Tracking: This application does not collect, track, buy, or sell your personal data. No third-party cookies, tracking pixels, or external analytics integrations are used.
  • Local Preference Memory: To enhance your user experience, your preferences (such as your last selected transit authority, lines, and stops) are saved directly inside your browser's local memory (localStorage). This data is entirely client-side, never leaves your device, and can be cleared at any time by wiping your browser's site data or history.
  • Transient Infrastructure Protection: Inbound network connections are analyzed dynamically in volatile server memory solely to mitigate cyber attacks, filter spam, and block automated malicious bots. These temporary traffic streams are evaluated in real-time for infrastructure security purposes and are never logged, written to persistent storage, or retained.
